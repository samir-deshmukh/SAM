How to use CETFind for Maharashtra CET college research

A practical guide to using CETFind to explore Maharashtra CET college options by percentile, city and course, with historical-data limitations explained.

CETFind is designed as a comparison tool: start with the information you know, narrow the public college records, then inspect the underlying historical data.

1. Start with your course

Choose a supported course and use the published directory or interactive search. Public coverage can vary by course and academic year.

2. Use percentile as a filter

Enter a percentile when the search supports it. Treat the resulting colleges as candidates for investigation, not guaranteed allotments.

3. Narrow by city

City filtering helps reduce the list to places relevant to your preferences. CETFind's public BBA directory also provides city-specific pages such as the Pune directory.

4. Inspect the college page

Open an individual college record to see the historical years and cutoff information represented in the public dataset. The page explains that these are historical records.

5. Verify before you act

Use the official CET Cell portal for the current year's eligibility, schedule, CAP instructions, seat availability and other admission rules. CETFind is not an official admission portal.
