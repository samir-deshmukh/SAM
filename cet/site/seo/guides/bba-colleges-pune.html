What the pages contain

  • College and city information represented in the public dataset.
  • Historical data years available for that record.
  • Historical cutoff summaries where the dataset contains them.
  • Links back to CETFind's interactive college finder.

Why CETFind does not rank colleges here

A simple “best college” ranking would require criteria that are not established by the published CETFind dataset. The directory therefore focuses on transparent records and comparison rather than pretending historical cutoff alone measures college quality.

Check current admission information

Pune college availability, CAP rules and current seat information can change by admission cycle. Verify current details with the official Maharashtra CET Cell and the college before making an application decision.
