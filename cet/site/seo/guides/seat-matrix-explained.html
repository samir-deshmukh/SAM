Maharashtra CET CAP seat matrix explained

Understand what a Maharashtra CET CAP seat matrix represents, how it relates to college choice, and why current official seat information matters.

A seat matrix is a structured view of seats published for an admission process. It provides context for college and course choices, but it is not a guarantee that a seat will remain available to a particular candidate.

What a seat matrix represents

The exact columns and categories depend on the course and admission publication. CETFind preserves seat-matrix information where it is represented in its supported public dataset.

Why seat data changes

CAP processes can have multiple rounds and changing availability. Seats can be allotted, accepted, cancelled or otherwise change status during an admission cycle.

How to use it with cutoff data

Cutoff history and seat information answer different questions. Cutoff history describes past admission outcomes; seat information provides context about published capacity. Neither alone predicts your future allotment.

For the current cycle

Always use the current official CET Cell publication for the authoritative seat matrix and CAP rules. CETFind's historical records are for research and comparison.
