Historical CET cutoff vs admission prediction

Why historical Maharashtra CET CAP cutoff data can help with college shortlisting but cannot reliably guarantee future admission.

Historical cutoff data is evidence about the past. Admission prediction is a claim about the future. CETFind deliberately keeps those two ideas separate.

What historical data can help with

  • Comparing past admission outcomes across colleges.
  • Seeing whether a college appeared more or less competitive in earlier records.
  • Building a shortlist for further research.
  • Understanding how outcomes varied across available historical records.

What it cannot guarantee

  • A future cutoff value.
  • A seat for a specific candidate.
  • A particular CAP-round allotment.
  • That the same eligibility, category or seat conditions will apply in a future year.

A better decision process

Use historical data as one input alongside the current official rules, your eligibility, current merit, preferences, location considerations and the live admission information for the relevant cycle.
