Understand the data before you choose.

CETFind helps Maharashtra CET candidates explore historical CAP cutoff and seat-matrix information. This page explains what the numbers mean, how the project uses published CAP records, and where official admission rules should be checked.

What is CETFind?

CETFind is a free college-finder and decision-support tool for Maharashtra CET candidates. It lets users explore historical admission outcomes and seat information across supported courses, including BCA, BBA, MBA and MCA.

The purpose is comparison and research: a candidate can examine past cutoff patterns, filter college options and inspect available seat-matrix information before making decisions.

What is Maharashtra CAP?

CAP refers to the Centralized Admission Process used for admissions covered by the Maharashtra State CET Cell. The exact process, dates, eligibility rules, documents, option forms, seat-allotment rules and reporting requirements can differ by course and academic year.

Because admission rules change, CETFind does not treat an old CAP guide as a permanent rulebook. For a current admission cycle, candidates should use the applicable official CET Cell portal, information brochure and notices.

Official source: Maharashtra State CET Cell, Government of Maharashtra ↗

Historical cutoffs

A cutoff shown by CETFind represents a published historical CAP outcome represented in the project's dataset. It can help answer questions such as how competitive a college or course was in a previous year.

Important: a historical cutoff is not a promised future cutoff and should not be interpreted as an admission guarantee.

Seat matrix

A seat matrix describes seats published for an admission process under the categories and conditions represented by the source material. Seat availability can change during CAP rounds.

Use the current official seat matrix and CAP instructions when making an actual application decision.

How CETFind uses the data

  1. Source: published Maharashtra State CET Cell CAP material is collected for supported courses and years.
  2. Extraction: relevant cutoff and seat information is extracted from the source records.
  3. Validation: imported records pass project validation checks before publication; problematic records can be held for review.
  4. Publication: approved public data is made available to the college-finder and result views.
  5. Interpretation: CETFind presents historical evidence for comparison; it does not turn historical data into a guarantee or official prediction.

How to use a cutoff responsibly

  • Compare several years rather than relying on one number.
  • Check the course, college, category, CAP round and other conditions attached to the record.
  • Remember that past demand does not determine the next year's merit list.
  • Check current eligibility, fees, seat availability and official admission instructions separately.
  • Use CETFind for research, then verify important decisions against current official sources.

Frequently asked questions

What is CETFind?

CETFind is a free web-based college-finder and decision-support tool for Maharashtra CET candidates. It helps users explore historical CAP cutoff and seat-matrix information across supported courses and compare college options.

Which courses does CETFind cover?

CETFind currently works with the project's published historical CAP dataset for BCA, BBA, MBA and MCA. Coverage can vary by academic year and available source records.

What does a historical CAP cutoff mean?

A historical CAP cutoff records an admission outcome from a previous CAP process for a particular course, college, category or other published condition. It is useful for understanding past trends but does not guarantee a future allotment.

Can CETFind guarantee admission to a college?

No. CETFind does not predict or guarantee admission. Future allotments depend on the current year's rules, applications, merit, seat availability, category and other official conditions.

Where does CETFind's admission data come from?

The project's cutoff and seat-matrix data is derived from Maharashtra State CET Cell CAP publications processed through the project's validation and publishing workflow. Current official CET Cell notices and portals remain authoritative.

Is CETFind affiliated with the Maharashtra State CET Cell?

CETFind is an independent project. It uses published Maharashtra State CET Cell CAP information as a data source and does not claim to be an official CET Cell portal or representative.

Official information takes precedence

CETFind is not an official government admission portal. When an official notice, information brochure or current CAP portal conflicts with historical information shown here, follow the current official publication.
