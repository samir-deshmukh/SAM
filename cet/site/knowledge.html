CAP Knowledge

This section explains common CAP concepts using official Maharashtra State CET Cell information. Rules can change by academic year, so always check the current course-specific CAP portal and notices.

Where the official rules come from

The Maharashtra State CET Cell publishes admission-process information, CAP portals, notices, information brochures and course-specific updates. This site treats those official publications as the authority for CAP rules.

Source: State CET Cell, Government of Maharashtra ↗

CAP process

The exact sequence, dates and candidate actions depend on the course and academic year. Use the current course admission portal for the authoritative schedule and instructions rather than relying on an old guide.

Official CAP/admission information: CET Cell portal ↗

Cutoffs and allotments

Our college results are based on the cutoff data imported into the CET CAP dataset. A historical cutoff is evidence about a previous CAP outcome; it is not a guarantee of a future allotment.

Data provenance: State CET Cell CAP publications used by the project.

Important

Do not use this knowledge page as a substitute for the current official CAP notice, brochure or portal. When an official notice changes a rule or date, the official notice takes precedence and our content should be updated.

Knowledge base is versioned by academic year in the project documentation.
